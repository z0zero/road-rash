# Difficulty is the race's one configuration besides its seed

v1.0.0's race is fair to a player who rides a decent line or hits back, but a first-timer rides at the back alone, and a player who has qualified wants more. Rather than make the one race easier for everyone, the game offers a difficulty, Easy, Normal or Hard, chosen on the title screen, and the Race module takes it with the seed: `createRace({ seed, difficulty })`. This is the race configuration ADR 0004 said would come when something needed one. A difficulty is a name, not numbers the game passes in: each is a small, fixed set of named tuning values in the Race module (the opponents' pace, how often they attack the player, their breathers, their patience and catch-up), so every race the game can create is one the Race module's tests can create, and the same seed, difficulty and inputs replay the same race. Only the opponents change: the track, the traffic and the cops are the same on every difficulty. Normal keeps v1.0.0's tuning and is the default: a race recorded with v1.0.0 replays on it byte for byte unless a later fix to the rules changes it, as those fixes change every difficulty alike, and `?seed=` replays take `&difficulty=`; a seed alone, as in v1.0.0's addresses, replays on Normal, whatever difficulty the player last chose. Each difficulty keeps its own personal best, since times on one don't compare with times on another, and a v1.0.0 save's personal best becomes Normal's. A difficulty must not grow into a career mode's settings, such as other tracks or upgrades: those would revisit this decision.
